# PAI motion assets

Final glass v2 media for the revised PAI public introduction at [PAI on Cloudflare Pages](https://pai-loop.pages.dev/). Asset version `20260907-glass-3` was published on 2026-09-07 as deployment `e81d8d1c-ba78-4855-8357-69e2a4c493c4`, superseding hotfix `22a71585-822b-45e7-a41f-347fd12852dd`. The final version was verified locally and on the production URL (1080p media, versioned assets, default-on motion, active judgment CTA, readable contrast and no console errors). First-revision CI passed; final CI is pending. The source PR remains unmerged to avoid triggering the separate Render application deployment.

The film combines actual PAI application captures containing synthetic demonstration data with flat 2D Paperlogy wordmarks, one simple physical glass object per scene, perspective camera moves, lighting and larger, high-contrast Korean captions. It includes close-ups of source evidence and the human decision controls. It is not a recording of production records or a real company's score, private evidence, participation decision or award result. Keep the visible example-data label.

Typography, glass geometry, lighting and camera movement were newly rendered at 2560×1440, then Lanczos-downsampled to 1920×1080 with Rec.709 limited-range output. The actual UI textures remain limited to their original detail: 1280×720 for the decision, evidence, requirements and score captures, and 1265×712 for the overview capture. This does not create native 1080p detail inside those UI textures, and no previous film was upscaled. The renderer avoids shadow-map and coplanar self-shadow setups from the earlier treatment.

The film opens and closes with a flat PAI wordmark. Between them, it shows discovering a notice, reading document conditions, comparing linked evidence and review information, and recording a person's decision. AI does not independently finalize scores or decide participation. Uncalculated and review-required states in the source UI must not be changed into claims of successful production analysis.

The page's four-step glass story is compiled from `story.html` by `build.mjs`. Build before previewing the complete page at `http://127.0.0.1:8791/`. The film uses native video controls and a static poster; reduced motion prevents automatic playback. The separate scroll story defaults to motion on even under the OS reduced-motion preference, as explicitly requested by the user. Its visible off control retains that choice across resizing. Small or short viewports and no-JavaScript pages show all four steps sequentially; desktop CTAs are interactive only on the sharp reading plateau.

Only the two final media files listed above are permitted in `dist/assets/`. The build removes previous `pai-loop-background.webm`, `pai-loop-poster.webp`, `pai-product-tour.webm`, `pai-product-poster.webp`, `pai-intro-3d.mp4` and `pai-intro-3d-poster.webp` from generated deployment output. WebM alternates, this README, local rendering dependencies, source screenshots and capture/QA inputs must not be deployed. The public page makes no application API calls and contains no private source material.

The local QA record is `.local/film/glass-v2-qa.json`; it contains final metadata, source-resolution limits and media hashes. QA artifacts include a decoded contact sheet, native-resolution glass close-up, 600px embedded preview, consecutive-frame crops and an animated close-up. Main captions are 76.5px at 1080p, or about 23.9px when displayed at 600px wide; supporting captions are 36px and 11.25px respectively. The final poster independently decodes at 1920×1080.

Final local page checks covered 320, 390, 1024, 1280 and 1440px layouts, full panel fit, keyboard End/Home, sharp-plateau CTA access, native playback and the persistent explicit motion-off choice during resizing. Dark text on light sections, larger page fonts and larger glass callouts are included in this revision.
