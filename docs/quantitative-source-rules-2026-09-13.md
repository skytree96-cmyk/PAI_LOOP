# Source-faithful quantitative rules and bounded extraction cost

Quantitative extraction describes an objective scoring program, including rules
written in prose, formulas, footnotes and referenced appendices. A physical grid
is not required. A missing structured program does not prove that the source has
no objective scoring rules.

## Executable source forms

- A `PERFORMANCE_COUNT` CASE row may use `BETWEEN` for an explicitly inclusive
  integer interval. `comparison_value` is its lower endpoint and
  `comparison_upper_value` its upper endpoint. The exact complete condition and
  award must be anchored in the source. Reversed endpoints, inferred bounds,
  overlapping intervals and numeric `IN` categories remain invalid. Unprinted
  counts remain unscorable.
- An explicit zero-point `미제출` row uses `NOT_SUBMITTED`, with no numeric bounds
  or categories. It is distinct from zero completed contracts, an absent fact,
  an unverified ledger or missing evidence. The engine accepts this state only
  from a confirmed input with evidence identity and the exact criterion binding;
  register derivation never creates it from missing data.
- Continuous numeric CASE programs may end in an explicit `LT` at exactly the
  last descending `GTE` boundary. This preserves a printed ratio's complementary
  lower interval without inventing an upper bound or rounding to an integer.
  Arbitrary continuous lower-tail gaps and overlapping `LTE` rows remain invalid.
- A shared scoring cell must have a proven source association with its named
  criteria. New operators do not repair lost table layout or establish ownership.
  A five-point subtotal is not two additive five-point criteria merely because
  two dimensions beneath it each contain a five-point band. A technical-score
  passing threshold does not become a minimum for its objective subtotal.

## Recognition conditions remain necessary

Numeric scoring rules do not prove that a company's records qualify. Performance
recognition now refuses automatic aggregation when a source requires participant
counts or annualized contract amounts that the register cannot verify. Both new
parsing and existing scope execution check these limitations; an unsupported
scope yields no numeric lower bound that could be promoted to maximum points.

An explicit `공고일 기준` anchors the lookback to the notice date. An explicit
`공고일 전일까지` completion clause excludes work completed on the notice date.
A stored scope whose date basis disagrees with its source is reviewed rather
than silently evaluated against the submission deadline.

An enterprise credit rating must keep its own column. Company-bond and commercial
paper aliases must not be combined with enterprise ratings to satisfy a row.
Company evidence still requires validity and the exact current criterion binding.

## Avoid unnecessary paid calls

The existing 20-character minimum now measures actual document content, excluding
generated document, page, section and container labels. The original text and
per-member audit remain unchanged. Empty or short archive members remain visible
as review conditions and cannot inflate a wrapper into provider-eligible input.
Supported archive documents containing enough actual text are still eligible.

The new contract is prompt `0.5.7`, schema `0.4.2`, validator `0.6.20`, processing
`0.5.1` after the original-document follow-up;
the earlier `0.6.19` version of this unmerged PR was not deployed.
Exact predecessor `0.5.6 / 0.4.1 / 0.6.18 / 0.5.0` records remain readable
as `LEGACY_CASE_V2`, alongside the existing `LEGACY_CASE_V1` contract. This is a
read policy, not a rewrite or a revalidation without original source text.

Predecessor proofs retain their original fingerprints and company-fact bindings
when the additive upper-bound field is absent. Populated upper bounds and new
operators cannot be smuggled under an old contract, including raw REVIEW rows.
Unknown tuples, invalid fingerprints, stale manifests and newer-generation
barriers continue to block reuse. New writes require the current contract.

Whole-requirement extraction still sends ordinary documents in full. A future
quantitative-only route must preserve parent subtotals, decision-bearing prose,
formulas and referenced recognition conditions, while keeping its coverage and
cache identity distinct from complete requirement extraction. Filename-based
exclusion of all scopes, forms, ZIPs or qualitative-looking grade labels is not
safe. No paid backfill, production migration or deployment is implied by these
code changes.

## Verification

Synthetic regressions cover source-to-record-to-manifest-to-bound-engine scoring,
integer interval edges and gaps, continuous ratio boundaries, explicit submission
state versus missing facts, unsupported performance dimensions and date cutoffs,
zero provider calls for label-inflated short content, useful archive text, exact
predecessor reuse, fact-binding stability and stale/vocabulary rejection.
Private procurement documents and company ledgers are not test fixtures.

## Original-document follow-up

Distinct financial ratios can share the canonical metric while retaining separate
source-bound scopes and fact bindings. Activation accepts this only when each
ratio has an explicit, distinct financial scope; unknown, composite and duplicate
scopes remain ambiguous. Fact selection and merging use metric plus criterion
binding, so an equity ratio cannot overwrite a current ratio or suppress its
register-derived value.

Participant thresholds and annual-contract requirements are preserved as
`manual_verification_conditions` in the recognition scope. A mechanically valid
scoring rule can reach the engine before company evidence is available. The
performance register still returns REVIEW with no numeric bounds for those
conditions. Only a separately verified, exact-criterion-bound aggregate company
fact can supply the missing count; an overview, a large register or absent
documents cannot establish it.

A deliberate exclusion note is ignored for objective scoring only when its
complete grammar identifies qualitative/price subjects, their matching methods,
and their exclusion from the objective table. Actual missing content, mixed
objective subjects and additional absence claims remain blockers. Full manifest
coverage requirements are unchanged.

Dense source rows may recover context only through bounded structural proofs:
an exact ordered count program immediately follows its unique source recognition
clause, or complete credit rows follow the unique ordered bond/paper/enterprise
header and precede their terminal footnote. The original quote is preserved;
operators, categories, thresholds, awards and row order never change. Unproven
column selection, missing categories, duplicate anchors and overlapping claims
remain failures. The score engine consumes the same source proof as validation.
